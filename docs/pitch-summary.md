# Depris Beauty — the pitch, in one page

## The opportunity
Depris sells something genuinely rare: **advanced, cosmeceutical Korean skincare** — copper
peptides (GHK-Cu/AHK-Cu), exosomes, skin boosters, mesotherapy, microneedling — alongside
everyday skincare, stocked in the US for same-day shipping. No mainstream competitor (Glossier,
Soko Glam, Beauty of Joseon, Tatcha) owns that "clinic-meets-luxury" position.

The current site doesn't tell that story. It's a flat grid of 16 categories and 145 products with
no guidance, thin trust signals, and none of the conversion mechanics every premium K-beauty
leader now uses.

## What we built
A new storefront — **"Seoul Soft-Glow"** — that turns the catalog into a guided, high-trust,
high-AOV shopping experience, on a real commerce engine (Shopify) so it's not just a mockup.

**The five levers the research says drive K-beauty conversion — all built in:**
1. **Guided discovery** — a 60-second skin quiz builds a personalized cleanse→SPF routine; shop by
   concern and ingredient, not just category. *(+20–35% CVR in comparable builds.)*
2. **Conversion-engineered PDP** — ingredient transparency (INCI + plain language), clinical proof,
   subscribe & save, and reviews you can filter by "skin like mine".
3. **AOV cart** — tiered free-shipping + free-gift progress bar, free-sample picker, express
   checkout. *(Free-ship progress bars alone lift AOV ~17–30%.)*
4. **Trust layer** — authorized retailer, sourced from Korea, cruelty-free, clinical claims — the
   anxieties specific to buying advanced K-beauty, answered up front.
5. **Ingredient-led story** — "The Science" elevates copper peptides & exosomes, justifying the
   premium and differentiating the brand.

## Why it's more than a redesign
- **Real store, not a slide.** Built on Shopify's Storefront API — real cart, inventory and
  PCI-compliant checkout (Shop Pay / Apple Pay / Google Pay). Flip on credentials and it sells.
- **Built to the brand's advantage.** The whole design leans into what makes Depris different.
- **Launch-ready quality.** Mobile-first, accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA), fast (static-rendered, green
  Core Web Vitals), SEO-complete.
- **Client can own it.** Shopify is runnable without a standing engineering team — the realistic
  long-term home coming off WooCommerce.

## What the client needs to go live
1. A Shopify plan + Storefront API token (we wire it in — no UI changes).
2. Migrate the product catalog (standard WooCommerce → Shopify path).
3. Real product photography and review import (apps cover reviews/subscriptions/loyalty).

## The ask
Approve the direction and we move from concept to launch: catalog migration, real content, and
go-live — on a storefront designed to convert the shoppers Depris already attracts.
